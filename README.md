# AION 2 Energy Reminder

**Track your characters' energy — all in one place.**

A lightweight Windows application for tracking Odyle Energy regeneration in AION 2. Keep an eye on multiple characters, monitor energy recovery, and know exactly when each character will reach full energy.

## Screenshots

![AION 2 Energy Reminder - Main Window](https://i.postimg.cc/PxQLfqnK/main-window.png)

## Features

- **Modern UI** — completely redesigned sleek interface built with CustomTkinter.
- **Custom Accent Colors** — personalize the app with 7 presets or choose your own color.
- **Adjustable Card Sizes** — choose between Compact, Medium, or Large layouts to fit your screen.
- **Quick Spend Buttons** — easily subtract -40 or -80 energy with a single click.
- **Multiple Characters** — track energy for all your characters in one place.
- **Automatic Regeneration** — calculates +15 energy every 3 hours.
- **Full Energy Prediction** — see exactly when each character will reach maximum energy.
- **Two Energy Limits** — supports 560 and 840 maximum energy.
- **Dark and Light Themes** — choose your preferred appearance.
- **Multilingual Interface** — English and Ukrainian languages.
- **Automatic Saving** — character data and settings are saved locally.

## Download

Download the latest version from the [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/tapcoin598-cloud/AION-2-Energy-Reminder/releases) page.

1. Download `AION-2-Energy-Reminder.exe`.
2. Run the executable.
3. Add your characters.
4. Start tracking your energy!

**No Python installation required.**

## Data & Privacy

Character information and application settings are stored locally on your computer.

The application does not require an account or game login.
